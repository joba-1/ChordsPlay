What you need for what

With any speaker, headphones or amp: playing the songs, the chords as large as the screen allows, PDF tabs, choosing songs (filter), tags, the song on a TV or tablet. The sound comes out of the device this page runs on, so connect it to whatever you play through: headphones, a speaker, the AUX input of an amp, or a Mustang Micro Plus by Bluetooth.

Only with a Fender Mustang Micro Plus: the guitar sound changing by itself for each song (and within a song), equal loudness of all sounds, adjusting effects, and recording takes. For these, the computer the Mustang is connected to needs amp control, set up once (see Guitar tone below).

With a separate guitar amp: everything in the first paragraph works. The guitar sound is then set on your amp, ChordsPlay doesn't change it.

Show the song on a TV, tablet or second screen

The other screen only shows the song, large, and follows this page: next song, pause, PDF pages. It plays no sound and can't change anything.

  1. Press d here (or Display).
  2. Tablet, phone, laptop: scan the QR code, or open the link shown there in that device's browser. It works for 12 hours.
  3. Your own TV: install a browser on it (on Google TV e.g. TV Bro), open once and keep it as a bookmark. The TV shows a code: enter it here with d under Pair a TV. From then on just open the bookmark on the TV.
  4. On the TV, press OK on the remote (or click the screen) for full screen.
PDF tabs

A song can show a PDF (e.g. a tab printed from Ultimate Guitar, or your own sheet) instead of its chords.

  1. Save the PDF in the song's folder on the music share, with the song file's name: for 16 We Will Rock You.mp3 name it 16 We Will Rock You.pdf. A second one for a part: 16 We Will Rock You - Solo.pdf.
  2. Press v to switch between the chords and the song's PDFs. ChordsPlay remembers the choice per song.
  3. Page Down / Page Up turn the pages; a TV or tablet turns along.
  4. V (or PDF) lists all PDFs. For a file that wasn't found by its name, choose its song there.
Guitar tone (Mustang Micro Plus)

Press g (or Tone) to choose the Mustang's sound for this song: from the start, from a point in the song, or for all songs of the artist. Level makes all sounds equally loud (matched), or louder or quieter, e.g. +3 for a solo. Knobs adjust effects while the song plays; Save keeps them with the song. G keeps the sound the Mustang has right now (chosen with its own buttons) for this song.

Amp control, set up once: the Mustang is switched by the computer it is connected to by Bluetooth (and USB for takes). On that computer:

  1. Open this page there, press g, then Set up amp control…. Copy the line it shows.
  2. Ask the person who looks after your computers to put that line into the amp control's settings and start it (instructions: docs/web.md, "Amp bridge"). It then starts by itself with the computer.

The top right shows what the Mustang does. If it says amp control off, that computer is off or amp control isn't running there. Mustang not connected: switch the Mustang on and connect it to that computer by Bluetooth. Mustang idle is fine: after 5 minutes without a change the Mustang saves battery; sound is not affected, and the next change wakes it.

Recording takes (Mustang Micro Plus by USB)

Press r to record yourself while a song plays, r again to stop. The take holds the song and your guitar, as you hear them. R lists the song's takes to listen to or delete. The Mustang must be connected by USB to the amp control computer.
